Privacy Policy

Blip Locator  ·  Last updated: March 2026

Blip Locator is a Bluetooth device finder app built for personal and family use. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data we collect

None. Blip Locator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

Permissions used

The app requests the following Android permissions:

Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) — used to scan for nearby Blip devices and to advertise the device's presence to other Blip users in the same household.

Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) — required by Android to perform Bluetooth Low Energy scanning. The app does not access, record, or transmit your location. This permission is a system requirement for BLE on Android and is used solely to enable Bluetooth scanning.

Foreground Service / notifications — used to keep the Bluetooth listener running in the background so the device can be found even when the screen is off.

Bluetooth data

The app detects nearby Bluetooth devices in order to display signal strength and device type. Non-Blip devices are shown only as anonymous type labels (e.g. "Headphones", "Bluetooth Device") — raw device names and MAC addresses are never displayed or stored beyond the current session.

Third-party services

Blip Locator uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or cloud services of any kind.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. No data is collected from any user.

Changes

If this policy changes in a future update, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
