<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<p class="meta">Blip Locator &nbsp;·&nbsp; Last updated: March 2026</p>

<p>Blip Locator is a Bluetooth device finder app built for personal and family use. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.</p>

<h2>Data we collect</h2>
<p><strong>None.</strong> Blip Locator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.</p>

<h2>Permissions used</h2>
<p>The app requests the following Android permissions:</p>
<p><strong>Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT)</strong> — used to scan for nearby Blip devices and to advertise the device's presence to other Blip users in the same household.</p>
<p><strong>Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)</strong> — required by Android to perform Bluetooth Low Energy scanning. The app does not access, record, or transmit your location. This permission is a system requirement for BLE on Android and is used solely to enable Bluetooth scanning.</p>
<p><strong>Foreground Service / notifications</strong> — used to keep the Bluetooth listener running in the background so the device can be found even when the screen is off.</p>

<h2>Bluetooth data</h2>
<p>The app detects nearby Bluetooth devices in order to display signal strength and device type. Non-Blip devices are shown only as anonymous type labels (e.g. "Headphones", "Bluetooth Device") — raw device names and MAC addresses are never displayed or stored beyond the current session.</p>

<h2>Third-party services</h2>
<p>Blip Locator uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or cloud services of any kind.</p>

<h2>Children</h2>
<p>The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. No data is collected from any user.</p>

<h2>Changes</h2>
<p>If this policy changes in a future update, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.</p>
